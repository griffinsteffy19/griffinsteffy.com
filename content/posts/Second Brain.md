    # Notes
    
    - Technology should be an extension of yourself, how does the technology you use extend your capabilities, and not hinder them?
    - Productivity Technology Phases
        1. 90s - Email
        2. 00s - task lists
        3. 10s - digital notes
        4. 20s - ????
    - Theory is that apps like Notion/Obsidian are the next phase of productivity apps
    - The previous generation doesn’t become unnecessary, but rather more efficient
    
    **Working Documents**
    
    - essential for action
    - change quickly
    - different ways to interact/view
    
    [SOP](https://www.notion.so/SOP-df43b9bb65504411b4a5fa91f09e54db) 
    
    - things that repeat and are standardized
    
    *Organize for Actions that are Important*
    
    *Low Effort is Key for a tool to become useful*
    
    ie. Solution has to be better than the setup time
    
    **“***Systems that must be **perfect** to be **reliable** are deeply flawed*”
