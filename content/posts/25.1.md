# What is 25.1
Every year CrossFit puts out a worldwide challenge of a workout a week for a handful of weeks (typically 3 as of late). This competition started last week, and the first workout was released on 20250227. The workout is
## 25.1
As many rounds and reps as possible in 15 minutes of:

3 lateral burpees over the dumbbell  
3 dumbbell hang clean-to-overheads  
30-foot walking lunge (2 x 15 feet)

*After completing each round, add 3 reps to the burpees and hang clean-to-overheads.

♀ 35-lb (15-kg) dumbbell  
♂ 50-lb (22.5-kg) dumbbell

More Information [here](https://games.crossfit.com/workouts/open/2025)

# My Effort
I first attempted 25.1 on Friday (20250228) and I honestly was pretty happy with my score, but as I went through the weekend, I felt like I could do slightly better. My main goal was to eliminate long rests, and stay more consistent on the movements.

What is cool is that I think I actually achieved that. Besides focusing on that I really didn't do anything special besides be a bit more mentally tough, and not trying to go too fast.
